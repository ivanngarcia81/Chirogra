Privacy Policy

Updated November 26, 2026 (version 1.4: the assistant)

In short: your books live on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your private iCloud account. No one at Chirogra can see them. Only if you use the assistant, your question and the figures needed to answer it go through our server to Anthropic, which makes the AI model; we don’t store them. We don’t use analytics or ads, and we don’t track you.

Who we are

Chirogra is a bookkeeping app for small businesses on iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what happens to the information you record in it. If you have questions, write to soporte@chirogra.com.

Your books stay with you

  • The information you record (company, customers, vendors, invoices, expenses, receipts, journal entries and reports) is stored on your device.
  • If you turn on Sync with iCloud, your data travels encrypted to the private database of your iCloud account to reach your other devices. Apple manages that database under its privacy policy; we have no access to it.
  • No one at Chirogra can see, read or copy your books.

The assistant

The assistant uses artificial intelligence to answer questions about your business and about using the app, and to prepare drafts (an expense, an invoice, a payment, an appointment) that you review and save. The app asks for your permission before you use it for the first time. If you don’t use it, nothing is sent.

  • What is sent: what you type and, when needed to answer, totals your device calculates (for example, this month’s expenses by category, open invoices, or the name of a customer or vendor). Also your company name, the date, the language and whether you use iPhone, iPad or Mac. Your full books never leave your device.
  • Where it goes: through Chirogra’s server (on Supabase, in the United States) to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude model. It travels encrypted.
  • What is stored: our server doesn’t store the conversation. It only counts how many questions you ask each month, for the subscription limit, using the purchase number the App Store provides (or, during free use, the app’s), without your name or email. Anthropic doesn’t use what it receives to train its models and keeps it only for a limited time for safety and abuse prevention, under its commercial terms.
  • What you save: an assistant draft only goes into your books when you tap “Save.”

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don’t share your data with third parties, except what you send to the assistant when you use it.
  • We don’t sell information.

Device permissions

  • Face ID or Touch ID: if you turn on the lock, the system checks your identity. Chirogra never receives your biometric data.
  • Photos and files: only when you choose a photo or PDF (a receipt, your company logo, a W-9). They are stored inside your books.
  • Mail: when you send an invoice, Chirogra opens your device’s mail app; you send the message from your own account.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, appointment reminders and the daily notice summary are scheduled on your device. They don’t go through any server.
  • Calendar: only if you turn on “Add appointments to Calendar” in the agenda. Chirogra writes your appointments to its own calendar on your device.

Backups and exports

Backups (.chirogra files), PDFs and CSV files are created by you and saved where you choose. They are not sent to us.

Subscription and payments

The subscription is purchased and managed through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. Apple gives us sales reports that don’t identify you.

Crash reports

If you agree in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports so we can fix bugs. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app deletes the data stored on that device.
  • You can delete your iCloud data in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Chirogra (on Mac, System Settings › your name › iCloud).

Children

Chirogra is designed for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If we add features that change what is described here (for example, connecting your bank), we will update this page before launching them and change the date above. Version 1.4 added the assistant.

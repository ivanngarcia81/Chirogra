**Description** (4000 máx.)
Chirogra is complete bookkeeping for your small business, built for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Send invoices, record expenses, track sales tax and get the reports your accountant needs, without spreadsheets.

INVOICES AND ESTIMATES
• Professional invoices with your logo, colors and sales tax
• Estimates that turn into invoices, in full or as a deposit
• Email invoices and reminders, record full or partial payments

EXPENSES AND BILLS
• Record expenses with a photo or PDF of the receipt

SALES TAX

REPORTS
• Profit and loss, balance sheet and trial balance

• Sales tax summary

• Double-entry bookkeeping with a ready-made chart of accounts
• Journal entries, general ledger and period close

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-in
• Your books live on your devices and, if you choose, in your private iCloud account

MADE FOR APPLE DEVICES

SUBSCRIPTION
Chirogra includes a 14-day free trial, then a monthly subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings. If your subscription ends, your books stay available in read-only mode, and you can still view them and make backups.

### Plan para contadores (versión 1.1, varias empresas)

Segundo producto del **mismo grupo «Chirogra»**, de **nivel superior**: en App Store Connect › Subscriptions › Chirogra ›
«Edit Subscription Levels», ponerlo **por encima** de «Chirogra mensual» (nivel 1 = el más alto). Así, quien tiene el plan
normal ve «Mejorar» y Apple prorratea el cambio; bajar de plan se aplica al renovar. La app lee el plan de la suscripción
vigente y fija el límite de empresas: plan normal **1** (las archivadas cuentan), contadores **sin límite** (decisión del 8-nov; antes 5). Mientras el
producto no exista en App Store Connect (pero sí el plan normal), el límite es 1 y al pasarlo solo se avisa: **crearlo y
mandarlo a revisión con la 1.1**, o nadie podrá tener más de una empresa. Sin ningún producto (antes de publicar, copias
de prueba) no se limita.

## App Privacy

**Data Not Collected.** Chirogra no recoge datos: no hay servidores propios, ni analíticas, ni publicidad. Los datos del usuario
quedan en el aparato y en su iCloud privado (Apple), al que el desarrollador no tiene acceso. Los informes de fallos que
Apple comparte con permiso del usuario no cuentan como recogida por la app.

`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` en `Info.plist`: solo HTTPS y el cifrado del sistema.

## Notas para la revisión (App Review)

> Chirogra doesn't require an account. On first launch, tap "Try with sample data" to explore a company with invoices,
> expenses, sales tax and reports, or create your own company. The 14-day free trial starts from the subscription screen
> shown after setup. If the subscription ends, books stay in read-only mode (view, export and back up only). iCloud sync
> is optional (Settings › iCloud, backups and security). All data stays on the device and in the user's private iCloud
> database; there is no server.

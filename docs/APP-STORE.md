## English (U.S.)

**Subtitle** (30 máx.)
Bookkeeping for small business

**Promotional text** (170 máx.)
New: bank import and reconciliation, receipt scanning, cash flow and budgets, and a plan for accountants with unlimited businesses. Try it free for 14 days.

**Description** (4000 máx.)
Chirogra is complete bookkeeping for your small business, built for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Send invoices, record expenses, track sales tax and get the reports your accountant needs, without spreadsheets.

INVOICES AND ESTIMATES
• Professional invoices with your logo, colors and sales tax
• Estimates that turn into invoices, in full or as a deposit
• Email invoices and reminders, record full or partial payments
• Credit notes and customer balances
• A Pay Now button and QR code on invoices, with your own payment link

EXPENSES AND BILLS
• Scan receipts: Chirogra reads them on your device and fills in the expense
• Keep the photo or PDF of each receipt with its expense
• Vendor bills, scheduled payments and batch payments
• Recurring expenses and bills
• 1099 vendors with their W-9
• Reclassify expenses in bulk

BANKING
• Import bank and card statements in CSV, OFX or QFX
• Review every transaction in one inbox, with rules and possible matches
• Reconcile your accounts against the statement

SALES TAX
• Rates by state, county, city and district, combined into groups
• Exempt customers and prices with tax included
• What you collected and what you owe each agency, by period

REPORTS
• Profit and loss, balance sheet and trial balance
• Cash flow, and reports on a cash or accrual basis
• Budgets vs. actual
• Deductible expenses and an Export for My Accountant package
• Receivables and payables aging
• Sales tax summary
• Export to PDF and CSV for your accountant

REAL ACCOUNTING
• Double-entry bookkeeping with a ready-made chart of accounts
• Journal entries, general ledger and period close
• Posted entries are never edited: changes are reversed and recorded again

FOR ACCOUNTANTS
• Chirogra for Accountants plan: unlimited businesses, each with its own books
• A Clients dashboard shows who needs attention: sales tax due, bank transactions to review, overdue invoices and reconciliations
• Switch businesses from the sidebar or the business picker
• Books and receipts for every business are stored in your iCloud account and use its storage

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-in
• Your books live on your devices and, if you choose, in your private iCloud account
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Back up your books to a file you keep wherever you like
• Lock the app with Face ID or Touch ID

MADE FOR APPLE DEVICES
• Native app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, synced with iCloud
• Mac tables with an inspector, keyboard shortcuts and drag and drop
• English and Spanish

SUBSCRIPTION
Chirogra offers two monthly subscriptions, each with a 14-day free trial: Chirogra Monthly, for one business, and Chirogra for Accountants, for unlimited businesses. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings. If your subscription ends, your books stay available in read-only mode, and you can still view them and make backups.

## App Privacy

**Data Not Collected.** Chirogra no recoge datos: no hay servidores propios, ni analíticas, ni publicidad. Los datos del usuario
quedan en el aparato y en su iCloud privado (Apple), al que el desarrollador no tiene acceso. Los informes de fallos que
Apple comparte con permiso del usuario no cuentan como recogida por la app.

## Clasificación por edad

Responder «None / No» a todo (sin violencia, contenido sexual, juegos de azar, compras de bienes regulados, contenido generado
por usuarios ni navegador web) → **4+**.

## Exportación (cifrado)

`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` en `Info.plist`: solo HTTPS y el cifrado del sistema.

**Notas para la revisión de la 1.1 (App Review Information › Notes)**

> Chirogra doesn't require an account. On first launch, swipe through the welcome tour (or tap Skip) and, on the last
> page, tap "Try with sample data" to explore a company with invoices, expenses, banking, sales tax and reports, or tap
> "Create my company". The 14-day free trial starts from the subscription screen shown after setup. If the subscription
> ends, books stay in read-only mode (view, export and back up only).
>
> New in 1.1: the "Chirogra for Accountants" subscription (com.chirogra.app.contador.mensual), a higher level in the
> same group as "Chirogra Monthly". The regular plan allows one business (the sample business counts). To see the
> Accountants plan: Settings › Businesses › Add Business… (on iPhone: More › Businesses ›
> Add Business…). After subscribing to it,
> you can add more businesses, and the Clients dashboard appears at the top of the sidebar (on iPhone it is the first
> screen).
>
> Scan Receipt (Expenses) reads receipts on the device with Apple frameworks; nothing is uploaded. Bank statements are
> imported from CSV, OFX or QFX files the user chooses; there is no bank connection. iCloud sync is optional (Settings ›
> iCloud, backups and security). All data stays on the device and in the user's private iCloud database; there is no
> server.
